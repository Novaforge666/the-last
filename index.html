Data source

The last 1000 ticks are requested with ticks_history (end: "latest", style: "ticks") and then kept current with a ticks stream. All percentages are calculated in your browser from those real ticks — nothing is simulated. Synthetic indices are statistically independent per tick, so past frequencies are descriptive, not predictive.
