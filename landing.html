Everything in one screen

Five workspaces, one page, zero build step.

Manual Trade

Live tick, chart and last-digit strip with a contract ticket. Over, Under, Even, Odd, Matches, Differs, Rise and Fall — quoted together, bought in one tap per tick.

DBots builder

Import a Deriv Bot .xml, or build one in a four-step dialog. It writes a real, exportable file — and refuses to run any block it cannot execute rather than guessing.

Auto Bots

Four strategies — digit streak, frequency threshold, even/odd imbalance, martingale recovery — with take profit, stop loss and per-bot money management.

Tick analysis

Digit distribution over 25 to 1,000 ticks, even/odd split, streaks and an over/under probability table. Every percentage is measured from real ticks in your browser.

Entry points

Six over/under signal cards scored by your own streak and cold-zone rules, plus a scan that ranks all ten volatility markets. Describes the past window — never a prediction.

Zero install

One HTML file, no bundler, no framework, no npm. Fonts are the only external asset; the chart is drawn on a canvas and the favicon is inline.

How you get in

Three steps, and the password step is Deriv's, not ours.

Sign in on Deriv

Press “Log in with Deriv”. You are sent to Deriv's own OAuth screens and authorise the trade scope.

Return and pick an account

Deriv sends you back with a single-use code, exchanged by a server-side function. Demo is chosen by default; a real account always asks for explicit confirmation.

Trade or automate

The terminal opens with live prices. Trade by hand, run a strategy, or load a DBot file — all on the same live tick stream.
